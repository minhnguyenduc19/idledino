Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I restore my purchases on iOS?
If you reinstalled the game or switched to a new iPhone/iPad, open the in-game Shop and tap the "Restore Purchases" button. Your non-consumable unlocks (such as Remove Banner Ads or pack entitlements) will be verified through Apple StoreKit and RevenueCat and restored immediately.
2. I made a purchase but did not receive the items. What should I do?
First, check that your device is connected to the internet and restart the game. If items still do not show up, please email us at minhnguyen.de.vn@gmail.com with your Apple receipt or transaction ID from Apple, and we will resolve it promptly.
3. How is my game progress saved?
Idle Dino stores your gameplay progression (unlocked dinos, upgrades, resources) locally on your device storage so that you can play 100% offline anytime. Ensure you do not uninstall the app without backing up your device via iCloud if you wish to preserve local data.
4. How can I request account or data deletion?
Idle Dino does not require account creation or store your personal identification on external servers. To reset all local progress, simply clear the app data or uninstall the application. For questions regarding pseudonymous advertising identifiers, please check our Privacy Policy or email us.
5. The game crashes or freezes. How do I troubleshoot?
Please ensure your device is running iOS 15.0 or higher. Close background apps, ensure at least 500MB of free storage space, and restart the device. If the problem persists, please contact us detailing your device model and iOS version.
